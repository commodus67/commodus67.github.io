Every row is scored only after the real result is known. Each match's probabilities are frozen roughly 36 hours before kickoff — before line-ups, injuries and late market moves — and never revised, so this is a genuine out-of-sample record, not a backtest fit to the same data. Accuracy is the share of matches where the highest-probability outcome (home/draw/away) occurred; Brier score and log loss score the full probability distribution and reward calibration, not just picking a winner — lower is better on both, and the baselines below show what "no skill" looks like on this exact sample. The Kalshi comparison uses the bid/ask midpoint, de-vigged (normalized to sum to 100%), captured at the same freeze time as the model's prediction.
